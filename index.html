In Closed Testing

King Me

Checkers, rebuilt as a roguelike. Fight across board after board on The Road, clear bosses and their guards, hit the three goals on each board, and build an army with 100+ upgrades, wild weapons, Trump & Miracle cards, and assignable hats — from a Hard Hat shield to a Phoenix Hat that survives one fatal hit.

Checkers RoguelikeStrategySteam Flagship

Status: Closed test live on Google Play (178 regions) · Steam flagship in development
Win: clear or block every enemy · Lose: army gone, no legal move, or the clock runs out

In Development

Serpent's Quest

A gothic-romance match-3, rebuilt from scratch. Match gems across 6 depths and 12 chapters, take the Demonic Wager, spin the Wheel of the Serpent for 5 gems, and collect daily rewards as the story descends. Winnings pay only in gems and content — there's no cash-out.

Match-3Gothic RomanceStory Campaign

Status: Rebuild in progress in Godot — first full build (v0.1) complete with wager, wheel, chapters & daily reward
Note: Contains simulated gambling — adults only

In Development

Terrasweep

An isometric voxel roguelike in the style of Mamono Sweeper. Every minesweeper tile you reveal expands your territory — battle the monsters hiding under the board, harvest the resources you uncover, and conquer the castles and villages standing in your way. Runs are permadeath, but what you bank carries into the next expedition through meta-progression.

Art direction: Shadow of the Colossus × Monument Valley × Minecraft × Mamono Sweeper

  • Slices 1–4 shipped: a playable Godot build you can already sweep, fight and conquer in
  • Castle & village sieges — including a captive princess waiting in the castle
  • Ancient dragon boss every 5 regions
  • Expansive 3×3-section world spanning four biomes: the Wilds, Ashlands, Fungal Deep and Sunken Wards
Voxel RoguelikeMinesweeperPermadeathMeta-progression

Status: In Development — playable Godot build (Slices 1–4) · Not yet published on Google Play or Steam

About ToastyRaptor

One developer, five very different games, one name on every store page.

🦖 The studio

ToastyRaptor is an independent game studio based in Boston, MA. It makes games with strong, simple hooks and a lot of personality: an arcade T-Rex that fights a mothership, checkers that plays like a dungeon crawl, and a match-3 that makes a wager with you.

Games are built in Godot and shipped by ToastyRaptor itself — design, code, testing, and store launches all run through the one studio. Every title here started as a real, playable build tested by real players before it went anywhere near a store.

If a game on this page says it's live, it's live. If it says coming soon, it's honestly still being made.
